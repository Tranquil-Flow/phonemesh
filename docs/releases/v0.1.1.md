# v0.1.1 release notes (draft, not released)

Not released. Hashes, signer and sizes are filled in from the signed APKs at release time.

## Identity

- Android package: `org.phonemesh.app`; version **0.1.1**.
- Local history schema **19** (v0.1.0 was 15). Phones upgrade in place; nothing is cleared.
- Contact cards are version 2: the card signs the whole profile (name, colour, picture choice).

## New

- **Your picture.** Choose a colour on a wheel, then an emoji, a letter or a small photo. Contacts see it on
  every screen. A change reaches each contact behind your next message to them, at most once an hour, with no
  extra bubble or sound. A photo is at most 64 px and 1 KB, sent once to each contact, one new photo a day.
- **Radio time.** Under the draft and on the picture screen, the app says how long the radio will send, for
  example "About 7 seconds on the radio", from the exact packets it would send. It says it does not know when the
  radio's setting is unknown.
- **Recovery kit.** Settings → Recovery kit makes a recovery card (QR and 12 words) and a sealed copy of your
  contacts. On a new phone the kit lists your people again; each one is added again, because a new phone has a new
  identity. The recovery key is never stored on the phone. (Crypto review: OPEN.)
- **Fewer resends.** A message that is not confirmed is sent again after about 2 minutes, then waits twice as long
  each time up to about 4 hours, at most 24 times in three days. When your phone hears from the person again, it
  sends their missed messages within a minute or two.
- **Your phone shares the radio.** On its own radio a phone sends at most 180 seconds of private mail an hour.
- **Local hub.** The hub's radio queue counts seconds on air: its acknowledgements first, then short packets,
  then whoever used the least radio time; phones get at most 180 seconds an hour. A phone on the hub's Wi-Fi gets
  its mail there, and the hub does not send that mail by radio.

## Limits

- Real phones and radios were not used for v0.1.1 checks; see `docs/android-verification.md`.
- Radio settings profile (fewer automatic broadcasts) is not included.
- Private group chats are not included.
