## Identity

- Android package: `org.phonemesh.app`; version **0.1.1**, version code **2**. Android 8.0/API 26+; target API 35.
- Local history schema **20** (v0.1.0 was 15). Phones upgrade in place; nothing is cleared.
- Contact cards are version 2: the card signs the whole profile (name, colour, picture choice). A v0.1.1 phone and a
  v0.1.0 phone cannot add each other (no v0.1.0 phones are in use; decision 36).

## New

- **Your picture.** Choose a colour on a wheel, then an emoji, a letter or a small photo. Contacts see it on
  every screen. A change reaches each contact behind your next message to them, at most once an hour, with no
  extra bubble or sound. A photo is at most 64 px and 1 KB, sent once to each contact, one new photo a day.
  Photos go only through a local hub, never by radio (see Limits).
- **Radio time.** Under the draft and on the picture screen, the app says how long the radio will send, for
  example "About 7 seconds on the radio", from the exact packets it would send. It says it does not know when the
  radio's setting is unknown.
- **Recovery kit.** Settings → Recovery kit makes a recovery card (QR and 12 words) and a sealed copy of your
  contacts. On a new phone the kit lists your people again; each one is added again, because a new phone has a new
  identity. The recovery key is never stored on the phone.
- **Fewer resends.** A message that is not confirmed is sent again after about 2 minutes, then waits twice as long
  each time up to about 4 hours, at most 24 times in three days. When your phone hears from the person again, it
  sends their missed messages within a minute or two.
- **Your phone shares the radio.** On its own radio a phone sends at most 180 seconds of private mail an hour.
- **Local hub.** The hub's radio queue counts seconds on air: its acknowledgements first, then short packets,
  then whoever used the least radio time; phones get at most 180 seconds an hour. A phone on the hub's Wi-Fi gets
  its mail there, and the hub does not send that mail by radio.

## Limits

- Real phones and radios were not used for v0.1.1 checks; see `docs/android-verification.md`.
- **Security review.** Four AI review agents checked the new cryptography: recovery and saved copies, cards and
  profiles, groups, and signed updates. None found a break in the encryption, and every finding is fixed. No human
  cryptographer has reviewed v0.1.1 yet.
- Radio settings profile (fewer automatic broadcasts) is not included.
- Private group chats are not included.
- **Photos need a local hub.** Your photo goes out only while your phone reaches a local hub, and a contact gets it
  when their phone fetches mail from that hub. Contacts who never use that hub see your emoji or letter instead.
  This keeps the shared radio free for messages; a way to send photos further may come later.

## How it was checked

- Every module's tests and checks (`scripts/check.sh`), and the full emulator suite: 773 of 773 selected tests in 42
  batches on an Android 15 emulator. The 8 tests left out use real speech.
- The release APKs were built from the exported source ZIP of the release commit, in a fresh folder.
- Signed v0.1.0 was installed on the emulator, a language chosen, then signed v0.1.1 installed over it: it opened on
  the chats screen in that language, with no crash.
- Four AI review agents checked the new cryptography (see Limits). Their findings are fixed.
- Not checked: real phones and radios, physical range, battery.
