# v0.1.2 production candidate (not signed or released)

The [v0.1.2 emulator demo](https://github.com/Tranquil-Flow/phonemesh/releases/tag/v0.1.2-demo.1) is published.
The production APKs are not signed or published. [v0.1.1](v0.1.1.md) remains the latest stable release.
No production tag, `main` change or production release is authorized by the demo publication.

## Identity and owner gates

- Android package `org.phonemesh.app`, version **0.1.2**, code **3**, Android 8.0/API 26+, target API 35.
- History schema **22**: v0.1.1 was 20; private groups add 21 and saved chats add 22. A test migrates a
  version-20-shaped file; a signed release-to-release over-install still needs the owner's APK signature and a separate emulator check.
- Signed ceilings remain 16,901,233 bytes for arm64-v8a and 14,890,111 for armeabi-v7a, each 262,144 bytes over signed
  v0.1.1. Signed sizes and signed hashes do not exist yet and are not substituted with unsigned ones.
- The owner signs the frozen inputs locally with the established release identity. Never send a private key,
  keystore or passphrase to an agent. The public release-certificate SHA-256 is
  `ef8557c6133a62388c5263cc26552d4ed3b036acce267a0b841a57bbdf5ecdac`.
- Production hub updates stay off until a real public-key/baseline pair is returned from the owner's offline kit.
  If that pair is swapped in, the current signing inputs become stale and must be rebuilt.
- The demo pre-release has its own APKs, laptop ZIP, corresponding source and checksums. Its download hashes belong
  to that exact demo commit, not to later production candidates.

## New

- **Private group chats.** A group of up to 20 people you have added, encrypted for its members only. Start one with
  **Add someone → New private group**: choose people, name the group, pick a colour and a letter, emoji or photo, and tap
  **Create group**. Each person gets a card with **Join** and **Ignore**; nobody is in a group without tapping **Join**.
  - Each member sends the group their own name and picture. Someone you have not added is shown as **Not in your
    contacts**, and **Add to my contacts** is in **Group details**.
  - The person who makes the group is an admin. Admins can add people, make another member an admin, remove people,
    change the name and picture, and switch on **New members see recent messages**. The last admin to leave chooses the
    next admin.
  - Your messages show **Delivered to N of M**, from receipts the other members' phones send. M is the number of other
    members when you sent the message; you are not counted.
  - Pictures: a full picture, a member's photo and the group photo go only through a local hub. Members without that hub
    see a colour sketch, one radio packet.
  - **New members see recent messages** (off by default): an admin who adds people shares the last 7 days of messages, at
    most 50, through a hub only, never by radio. New members see them as "Shared by &lt;admin&gt;".
  - **Group code:** four pictures in Group details, worked out from the member list. Members compare them like safety
    pictures to check they hold the same list.
  - Each join, leave or removal gives the group a new secret key, which an admin sends to every member through that
    member's private chat session. Changes made within about a minute share one new key.
  - Design and review notes: [private group keys (draft GK4)](../specs/2026-10-27-private-group-keys-draft.md) and
    [the accounts specification](../specs/2026-10-14-accounts-design.md), decisions 66 to 76.
- **Save your chats.** **Settings → Recovery kit** offers **Contacts only** or **Contacts and chats**. For chats you choose
  which ones, the last 90 days or all history, and whether to include pictures and messages that were not sent or not
  confirmed. The app counts messages and size before saving, reads the file back after saving, and **Check it now** opens
  it with your card. Group messages are included as history.
  - Restoring puts the chats under **Saved chats**, apart from live chats, marked "From this saved copy". They are read
    only: they never send, speak or notify. The copy holds no live secret keys, so a restored phone is not back in its groups and has to
    be added again (decision 75). A phone keeps at most eight saved copies.
  - Design: [encrypted chat backup](../specs/2026-10-26-encrypted-chat-backup-design.md), section 5.11 for the limits kept.
- **Speech-pack updates through a local hub.** **Settings → Local hub → Updates** lists newer speech packs that a hub
  holds. The phone gets the hub's signed list over the hub's own Wi-Fi (never mobile data, Bluetooth or the radio), checks
  it against the update keys built into the app, and downloads a pack only after you tap **Download**. You can **Pause**
  and **Resume**. A finished pack is checked against its signed hash and replaces the old one only if it opens; if anything
  fails the old pack stays.
  - This candidate carries TEST roots. Release builds refuse them and say **Updates are not set up in this version.**
    Enabling production updates needs the owner's two public key lines and an offline-signed baseline, then a tested
    key-swap commit and a new build before APK signing.
  - For hub operators: `phonemesh-hub import-updates <stick folder>` puts a signed release from a stick into the hub, and
    `update-status` shows what the hub holds. Signing is done by the project's owner on an offline computer
    ([update keys](../update-keys.md); [hub guide](../../android/hub/README.md)).
  - Packs only. The app itself is still updated by installing a new APK.
- **Demo.** Phonemesh Demo has Kofi, Ama and Yaw take part in private groups, and its hub offers a signed "Demo voice"
  update made with keys that exist only inside the demo ([the demo guide](../demo.md)).

## Fixed

- **Hub first start.** The hub's TLS store password was random text that could begin with a dash, which Java's `keytool`
  reads as one of its own options; about one first start in 4,096 failed. The password is now hexadecimal.
- **Closing a hub link.** Closing a phone's hub connection while it was still setting up TLS could wait on the handshake. It
  now closes the plain connection first, so it stops at once.

## Limits

- No physical phones, microphones or real radios were used for these checks. Emulator tests do not establish range, battery life or radio capacity.
- **Security review.** Three AI review lanes checked the new code: groups (15 findings), full chat backup (15) and the phone
  side of updates (15). Every finding is fixed, with tests and, for the security checks, mutation tests. The lanes used
  the same model family, not a different one per lane. Two mutants survive on purpose in the update code (one shadowed by
  an earlier check, one a timing-only difference) and one more is equivalent. No human cryptographer has reviewed any of
  it.
- **Groups.** At most 20 people. An admin can change a group only while every other member is that admin's contact;
  otherwise the app says "Add &lt;name&gt; to your contacts first." Members need not know each other. A phone still waiting for
  a change says "Group change on its way. Sending paused." If two admins change a group at once, sending stops until an
  admin taps **Fix the group**.
- **Group claims.** "Delivered to N of M" counts what members' phones report. The author and time of a "Shared by"
  message are the admin's word. The admin also binds each member's signing key to them, and the app trusts that binding
  even when members' group codes match. Messages longer than about 126 bytes are not shared with new members. A member
  with a modified app could read the recent messages meant for a later joiner.
- **Two-person groups.** If the last admin of a two-person group leaves, the other person keeps a group with no admin and
  nobody is told.
- **Group pictures and photos need a local hub**, like v0.1.1 profile photos. Members who never use it see a sketch, or the
  emoji or letter.
- **Saved chats.** A copy walks the selected rows rather than freezing the whole phone at one instant: a message deleted while it was being made may be missing, and
  messages longer than 4,096 bytes are shortened and counted. Replies and reactions match by radio packet number inside one
  chat, so a sender that reuses a number could attach a reply to the wrong message. If the app is closed while the file
  picker is open, the save does not finish and says so; start again, since the card made for it opens nothing.
- Radio settings profile (fewer automatic broadcasts) is not included. Ewe and Kabiyè, TalkBack and quantum-safe keys are
  not included.

## How it was checked

- The full silent emulator selection on `232cadb` reconciled 857 passes and 11 explicit skips, with eight real-speech
  tests excluded. No missing, extra, conflicting or failing test selectors remained. This is not 876 passing tests.
- Version metadata and its two release-unit assertions were then changed in `fa49a8c`. Both wrong-version mutants
  were caught. The direct public-tree `scripts/check.sh` passed, and all three demo ABIs were built.
- The published demo APK was installed on an Android 15 arm64 emulator and independently read back byte-for-byte.
  A new typed group message reached **Delivered to 2 of 2**, and Kofi and Yaw replied; fresh crash/error logs were empty.
- The remaining production candidate adds only three accessible names (group chooser row, group member row, recent-history
  switch) and test-only qualification/capture code. Before the names were added, two behavioral assertions failed on their
  absence. Afterwards 11 focused instrumentation tests passed: nine group-screen tests and two accessibility tests
  (English and French, light and dark). The test wrapper expected nine and refused; the 11 passes were reconciled
  separately, so this is neither a fresh full-suite run nor a failed product run. All three independent name-deletion
  mutants then compiled and failed their intended missing-description assertions. Restored-source GREEN passed;
  the final focused run passed 12 tests, including the non-skipped secure-window check. All 50 expected light/dark
  fixture frames were pulled and visually reviewed. The exact-selector composite has 862 passes and 10 explicit
  skips across 872 selected tests, with eight speech exclusions; it is not a fresh broad run. The older broad suite
  is reused only for reconciled unchanged scope. See [Android verification](../android-verification.md).
- The owner signing kit must use the unsigned APKs from the isolated source-ZIP build, not whichever APK happens to be
  in a developer build folder. The kit's build receipt and checksum manifest bind the actual inputs.
- Not checked: owner-signed v0.1.1-to-v0.1.2 upgrade, physical phones/radios, real speech, physical range or battery.
  The real update-key round-trip remains owner-gated. Do not treat the demo-only pre-release as a production release.
